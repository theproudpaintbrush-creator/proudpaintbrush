# Project Spotlight — Bringing a Gray Fence Back to Life (Katy)

- **Post type:** What's new

- **Button / CTA:** Learn more → https://www.theproudpaintbrush.com/exterior-painting/fence-staining/katy

---

This Katy cedar fence had gone silver-gray and was starting to drink up moisture. We pressure washed it back to clean wood, let it dry, set the popped nails, then worked a penetrating semi-transparent stain into the grain — including the picket tops and post caps where rot starts first.

The result: warm color back, water locked out, and years added to the fence's life.

A stained fence isn't just better looking — it's protected. If yours is graying or feels dry to the touch, it's time.
