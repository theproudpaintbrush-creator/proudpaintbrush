A wood fence is one of the biggest unprotected wood surfaces on your property — and Fort Bend's sun and humidity are hard on it. Left bare, cedar and pine gray out and start drinking up moisture within a couple of seasons.

A quality stain and sealer locks out water, blocks UV, and brings back the warm tone you paid for. The best time to do it is before the peak summer heat sets in.

Quick test: if your fence looks silver-gray or feels dry and fuzzy, it's overdue.
