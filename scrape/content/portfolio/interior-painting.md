# Interior Painting Portfolio
Residential Interior Projects | The Proud Paintbrush
Our interior painting portfolio showcases real projects completed by The Proud Paintbrush, highlighting clean finishes, consistent preparation, and attention to detail across a wide range of interior spaces.
These examples are intended to help homeowners see the quality and style of our work and decide whether our approach is a good fit for their home.

## Interior Painting Projects
Below you’ll find examples of completed interior painting projects, including:

- Living rooms and open-concept spaces

- Kitchens and dining areas

- Bedrooms and nurseries

- Bathrooms and powder rooms

- Trim, doors, and ceilings

- Accent walls and feature areas

- Cabinet painting and built-ins

Each project reflects our focus on surface preparation, even coverage, and clean execution.

## What You’ll Notice in Our Interior Work
As you browse our interior portfolio, look for:

- Smooth, consistent finishes

- Clean cut lines and edges

- Proper prep on walls, trim, and ceilings

- Thoughtful color application

- Respect for the home during the process

These details are what allow interior paint to hold up over time rather than needing early touch-ups or repainting.
