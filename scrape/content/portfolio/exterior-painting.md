Residential Exterior Projects | The Proud Paintbrush
Our exterior painting portfolio highlights completed projects that showcase durable finishes, consistent preparation, and attention to detail across a wide range of exterior surfaces.

## Exterior Painting Projects
Below are examples of exterior painting projects completed by The Proud Paintbrush, including:

- Full exterior repaints

- Stucco, brick, and siding painting

- Limewash brick finishes

- Garage doors and exterior trim

## What You’ll Notice in Our Exterior Work
As you browse our exterior portfolio, pay attention to:

- Even coverage and consistent color

- Clean lines on trim and architectural details

- Proper preparation on stucco, brick, and siding

- Finishes designed to hold up to sun and moisture

- A cohesive, finished look across the entire exterior

These details are what allow exterior paint to perform well over time instead of failing prematurely.
