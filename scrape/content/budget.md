## Typical Painting Cost Ranges in Sugar Land

### Interior Painting

- Walls only: $2,000 – $10,000

- Walls, ceilings, and trim: Starting around $5,000, up to $20,000 for large or complex projects

- High ceilings: Closer to $1.50/sq ft for ceilings and $2.00/sq ft for walls

- Detailed trim or crown molding: Priced higher due to additional labor and precision work

What affects the cost:

- Square footage and number of stories

- Number of colors per room

- Need for drywall repairs or priming (e.g., light over dark colors requires color-blocking primer)

- How long it’s been since the last paint job

### Exterior Painting

- Small homes: $2,500 – $4,000

- Medium two-story homes: $4,000 – $8,000

- Large homes: $8,000 – $16,000+

Factors that influence price:

- Repairs needed (wood rot, termite damage, gaps in caulking, nail pops)

- Substrate type:

- Brick: Requires priming and back-rolling

- Stucco: Requires special back-rolling techniques

- Vinyl: Must use vinyl-safe formulas

- Accessibility and safety measures (scaffolding, ladders)

### Cabinet Painting

- Small kitchen, brush-and-roll method: Starting at $2,000

- Fine-finish spraying: Starts around $4,000 and can go up to $8,000–$10,000 for large, high-end kitchens

- Seasonal Discounts: Flexible scheduling during slower seasons (e.g., October–November) can save you money.

- Define your priorities – Decide on durability, aesthetics, or both.

- Get a detailed estimate – We provide itemized quotes so you see exactly where your money goes.
