## Why Exterior Painting Costs Vary So Much
If you’ve ever received two very different exterior painting quotes, this is why.
Exterior pricing is heavily influenced by preparation and environmental exposure — not just square footage.

- Surface condition: peeling paint, cracks, or damage

- Type of material: stucco, wood, brick, or siding

- Amount of prep required: scraping, sanding, caulking

- Sun exposure: west-facing homes fade faster

- Moisture and humidity: impacts adhesion and longevity

- Height and access: two-story homes require more time and equipment

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should you repaint a home exterior?
Most homes need repainting every 5–10 years, depending on exposure and materials.

### What exterior paint lasts the longest in Texas?
High-quality products like premium acrylic systems designed for UV and humidity perform best.

### Do you pressure wash before painting?
Yes. Proper cleaning is essential for adhesion and longevity.

### Can you paint over peeling paint?
No. Peeling areas must be scraped and prepped before painting.
