## What Drives Interior Painting Costs
If you’ve ever gotten two very different painting quotes, this is why.
Interior painting isn’t priced randomly — it’s based on how much prep, time, and detail your home actually requires.

- Wall condition: repairs, cracks, or previous damage

- Drywall work: patches, texture, or full repairs

- Ceiling height: higher ceilings increase labor and access needs

- Trim detail: crown molding, baseboards, and casing

- Number of colors: more colors = more time and precision

- Occupied vs empty home: working around furniture adds time
