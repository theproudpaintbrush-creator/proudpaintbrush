## What Drives Painting Costs
If you’ve ever gotten two very different painting quotes, this is why.
Painting isn’t priced randomly — it’s based on how much time, prep, and detail your home actually requires.
Several key factors determine where your project falls within the price range:

- Project size: Larger homes require more labor and materials

- Surface condition: Damage, peeling, or previous paint failure increases prep time

- Prep requirements: The more prep needed, the longer the job — and the better the result

- Number of colors and finishes: More variation adds time, complexity, and precision

- Paint products: Higher-quality systems cost more upfront but last significantly longer

- Detail work: Trim, doors, cabinets, and intricate areas increase labor

- Access and height: High ceilings, tight spaces, or multi-story exteriors impact efficiency
