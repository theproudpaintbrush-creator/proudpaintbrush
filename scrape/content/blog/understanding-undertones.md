# Do You Make These Color Mistakes?

May 1

Written By Chris Petkau

When searching for interior and exterior painters in Houston or Sugar Land, one critical concept every homeowner should understand is paint color undertones.
Whether you're painting your kitchen cabinets, bedroom walls, or the full exterior of your home, undertones can make or break the final look. Every surface in your home—from your flooring and countertops to your furniture and even your pets—has an undertone. If you're choosing paint colors based only on how they look in-store or online, you're likely to end up with clashing or mismatched tones in your home’s lighting.

#### Krypton by Sherwin-Williams
Krypton is a soft, tranquil blue with strong gray undertones. In north-facing rooms, those gray hues become more pronounced, creating a calming and neutral backdrop—perfect for bedrooms or home offices.

### Why Undertones Matter in Home Painting Projects
Two paint colors that seem perfect together on a Sherwin-Williams color strip may clash in real-life lighting. That’s because undertones reveal themselves under different lighting conditions and when paired with other elements in your space.
When planning your interior painting in Sugar Land, consider the undertones in your flooring, countertops, furniture, and lighting. For the best results, always test samples in your actual space before finalizing colors.
✅ Pro Tip from The Proud Paintbrush: Don’t view paint swatches in isolation. A white paint may appear neutral until it’s compared to another white—and suddenly you notice yellow, pink, or gray undertones.

#### Divine White by Sherwin-Williams
This warm white has subtle yellow undertones that create harmony when paired with cooler, blue-toned lighting. It’s a great choice for living room painting projects or any space where you want a cozy but neutral feel.

### What Is an Undertone?
Every paint color is made by mixing primary colors. The mass tone is what your eyes perceive immediately (e.g., blue, red, white), but the undertone is the subtle hue underneath—often revealed only when compared with a “true” color.
For example:

- A “blue” cabinet may have a green undertone.

- A “white” wall may have a pink undertone.
When placed together, green and pink undertones can clash—even if the mass tones (blue and white) initially seemed like a perfect match.

### Cool vs. Warm Undertones
Understanding warm and cool color temperatures is key when hiring a professional house painter in Sugar Land or exterior painting contractor in Houston.

- Warm undertones = red, yellow, and orange

- Cool undertones = blue, green, and purple

Lighting also affects undertones:

- North-facing rooms often pull blue tones

- South-facing rooms enhance warm, golden tones

- LED bulbs may enhance green or yellow undertones

This is why professional paint color consultations are crucial to getting your project right the first time.

#### Greek Villa by Sherwin-Williams + Meditation Time by Behr
In a recent jungle-themed nursery project, we combined Greek Villa (off-white with warm yellow undertones) with Meditation Time (a green with earthy brown undertones). The combination of warm and cool undertones resulted in a balanced, inviting room with just the right amount of character.

### How to Identify Undertones the Right Way
To spot undertones, compare the paint you're considering to a “true” version of that color from a color wheel. That green you loved? It may reveal hints of brown, yellow, or blue when held against a pure green. This method is one of the best ways to avoid surprises and ensure your new paint color harmonizes with your space.
