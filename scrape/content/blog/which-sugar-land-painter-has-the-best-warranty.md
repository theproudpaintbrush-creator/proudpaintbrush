## What This Means for Homeowners
If you’re comparing painters in Sugar Land, here’s what to look for:

- Written terms — never accept a verbal “promise.”

- Length of coverage — 2 years is standard; 3–4 years is excellent.

- Product tie-ins — premium paints often extend both durability and coverage.

- Company stability — a warranty is only good if the company will be around to honor it.

That’s why it’s important to choose a painter with both strong reviews and clear warranties.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1) What’s the standard painting warranty in Sugar Land?
Most reputable painters offer 2 years. Franchises like That 1 Painter go up to 3 years, while The Proud Paintbrush offers 2 years standard and a 4-year upgrade

### 2) Why do some painters not publish warranty details?
Some smaller contractors don’t want to commit in writing. Always ask for warranty terms upfront, and make sure they’re included in your estimate.

### 3) Is a longer warranty always better?
Not necessarily. A clear, enforceable warranty from a stable company is better than a vague promise of “lifetime” coverage. Look at both the terms and the company’s reputation
