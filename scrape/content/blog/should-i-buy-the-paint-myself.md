# Should I Buy The Paint Myself?

Jul 21

Written By Chris Petkau

Should You Buy Your Own Paint or Let Residential Painting Contractors Handle It?
Completing a house painting project has become easier with the abundance of information available online. Today, a DIY painter can quickly research paints and equipment, then purchase everything needed from a local paint store. Despite this, many homeowners still rely on residential painting contractors for their house painting needs. One common question they face is, "Should I buy my own paint or let the painting contractor handle it?"

Purchasing Yourself

By purchasing your own paint, you gain the freedom to choose any color, brand, and finish that suits your home. This approach also allows for flexibility and extensive online research. Alternatively, you can have professional residential painters select high-quality paint for your project, ensuring convenience and efficiency.

Benefits of Purchasing Your Own Paint
There are several advantages to buying your own paint. Firstly, you have control over the brand and cost. You can search for deals and discounts from various paint manufacturers, making your decision easier. By purchasing the paint yourself, you maintain more control over the entire project. Additionally, having your chosen paint at home ready to use when the professional house painters arrive can be comforting.

Benefits of Hiring a Painting Contractor to Supply Paint

Hiring residential painting contractors and having them supply the paint offers numerous benefits. It is effortless and saves time; simply communicate your preferences, and the contractors handle the rest. Cost is another crucial factor. 

Many residential painting companies provide a flat quote that includes the total price of paints needed for the interior or exterior house painting project. Most professional home painters also pass along the contractor discount they receive from local stores, making it cost-effective to have your contractor purchase the paint.
Conclusion
Allowing your residential painting contractor to handle the paint purchase offers convenience, saving you time and ensuring a streamlined process. As a painting company, we personally prefer that you leave the purchase of paint to us so that we can ensure everything is perfect for you as our client. This approach allows us to leverage our expertise in choosing the right materials and managing logistics, ultimately making your house painting project more efficient and hassle-free.
