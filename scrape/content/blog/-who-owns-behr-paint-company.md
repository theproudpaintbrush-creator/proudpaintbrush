## Who Owns Behr Paint?
Behr Paint Company is owned by Masco Corporation.
Masco acquired Behr in 1999. Since then, Behr has remained one of the most widely recognized paint brands for homeowners, especially because it is sold through The Home Depot.
Behr has built its reputation around accessibility, convenience, and value for homeowners who want a paint product they can purchase easily and apply without a complicated process.
That makes it popular. But popularity and convenience are only part of the story.

## What Is Masco Corporation?
Masco Corporation is a large home improvement manufacturer with a long history in the residential market. The company is known for owning brands connected to remodeling, renovation, plumbing, cabinetry, and hardware.
For homeowners, this matters because a large parent company can influence:

- product development

- retail availability

- price positioning

- distribution

- brand consistency

In simple terms, Behr benefits from broad retail visibility and strong consumer accessibility.

## Is Behr Paint a Good Brand?

Yes, Behr can be a good paint brand for the right situation.
Behr is especially popular for:

- homeowner repaint projects

- budget-conscious interior painting

- DIY updates

- quick refreshes

- easy access through Home Depot

Many homeowners like Behr because it is easy to buy, easy to understand, and available in several product tiers.
Some of the better-known Behr lines include:

### Behr Premium Plus
A more budget-friendly option often used for basic repaints and simple interior projects.

### Behr Ultra
A step up for homeowners looking for better coverage and added convenience.

### Behr Marquee
Known for stronger hide and marketed as a premium interior and exterior option.

### Behr Dynasty
Positioned as one of Behr’s higher-end offerings, with improved durability and stain resistance.
For some rooms and some budgets, Behr can be a practical solution. But that does not automatically make it the best choice for every project.

## When Behr Makes Sense
Behr may make sense when:

- you are repainting a guest room or secondary bedroom

- you want a homeowner-friendly product

- you are doing a smaller interior project

- budget is a major factor

- convenience matters more than having a contractor-grade system

If your goal is a basic refresh with reasonable performance, Behr can be a workable option.

## Behr vs. Sherwin-Williams vs. Benjamin Moore
Homeowners often need real guidance here.

### Behr
Behr is known for convenience, accessibility, and value. It is easy for homeowners to buy and is widely recognized because of Home Depot.

### Sherwin-Williams
Sherwin-Williams is commonly trusted by painting contractors because of product consistency, system options, and strong performance across a wide range of surfaces. It is often a strong fit for exterior painting, trim, and demanding environments.

### Benjamin Moore
Benjamin Moore is often chosen for premium interior finishes, refined color selection, and a higher-end look. It is a favorite for homeowners and designers who want a more elevated finish.
For many professional painting projects, especially in Texas, the question is less about which brand is famous and more about which specific coating is right for the job.
That is an important distinction.
A homeowner might ask, “Is Behr good?”
A professional asks, “What system will perform best on this exact surface under these exact conditions?”
That is the difference.

## What We Typically Recommend at The Proud Paintbrush

We have worked with many products over the years. In our experience, the best paint choice depends on the project itself.

### Interior walls
A quality, washable finish matters most. The right product depends on traffic, sheen, and how the room is used.

### Exterior painting
Texas sun, humidity, rain, and heat all affect performance. Long-term durability matters more here than convenience at the shelf.

### Cabinet painting
This is one of the biggest areas where product choice matters. Cabinets need coatings built for adhesion, hardness, cleanability, and wear resistance.

### Bathrooms and laundry rooms
Moisture resistance is a bigger factor here than in a standard living room or bedroom.
That is why we often lean toward premium systems from Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore for projects where durability, appearance, and longevity matter most.
That does not mean Behr has no place. It means the right paint depends on the right use case.
