## Why Prep Matters More Than Most Homeowners Realize

Even the best exterior paint can fail if the prep is weak.
This is one of the biggest misconceptions in exterior painting.
Homeowners often focus only on the brand of paint, but long-term performance depends just as much on the process before the paint ever goes on.

Proper prep can include:

- pressure washing

- surface cleaning

- scraping peeling areas

- sanding rough spots

- caulking gaps and joints

- priming where needed

- protecting non-painted surfaces

At The Proud Paintbrush, we believe prep is a process, not a step.
That is one of the biggest differences between a paint job that still looks strong years later and one that starts failing early.

## What Makes a Great Exterior Paint for Texas?
This is where many homeowners get tripped up.
The best exterior paint for Texas is not just about brand recognition. It is about how the coating performs under real Texas conditions.
Here is what matters most:

### UV Resistance
Texas sun is brutal on exterior coatings. A good exterior paint needs strong UV resistance to help reduce fading and surface breakdown.

### Color Retention
Dark colors and bold colors are especially vulnerable to fading. Better paint systems hold their color longer.

### Flexibility
Exterior surfaces expand and contract with heat and weather changes. A rigid coating is more likely to crack or fail.

### Moisture Resistance
Humidity, rain, and damp conditions can create major problems if the paint system does not seal and perform properly.

### Mildew Resistance
In humid climates, mildew protection matters—especially on shaded sides of the home.

### Surface Compatibility
Stucco, brick, siding, wood, and trim do not all behave the same. The right product should match the actual surface.

If you are trying to decide what exterior paint is best, start with these questions:

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best paint for exterior houses in Texas?
In our experience, some of the best exterior paints for Texas homes are Sherwin-Williams Duration, Sherwin-Williams Emerald, and Benjamin Moore Aura Exterior.

### What exterior paint lasts longest in Texas heat?
Higher-end systems with strong UV resistance and proper prep usually last the longest. Product choice and surface prep both matter.

### Is Behr good for exterior painting in Texas?
Behr can be a reasonable budget-friendly option, but for long-term performance in Texas weather, we usually prefer higher-end systems.

### What is better for stucco exteriors in Texas?
For many stucco homes, Sherwin-Williams Duration is a strong choice because of its durability and performance in harsh conditions.

### Does prep really matter that much for exterior painting?
Yes. Even premium exterior paint can fail early if the surface is not cleaned, repaired, caulked, and primed properly.
