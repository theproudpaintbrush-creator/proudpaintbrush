## Who Owns Benjamin Moore Paint Company?
Benjamin Moore is owned by Berkshire Hathaway, the multinational holding company long associated with Warren Buffett.
Berkshire Hathaway acquired Benjamin Moore in 2000. Since then, Benjamin Moore has continued to operate as a premium paint brand known for strong finish quality, rich color, and dependable performance in residential applications.
For homeowners, this matters because Benjamin Moore has stayed positioned as a higher-end paint brand rather than simply competing on convenience or lowest price.
That difference affects how many people view the brand and why it is often recommended for projects where appearance and long-term value matter.

## What Is Berkshire Hathaway?
Berkshire Hathaway is a large holding company with ownership in many well-known businesses across multiple industries.
For most homeowners, the main takeaway is simple:
Benjamin Moore is backed by a major parent company with long-term financial stability, and the brand has maintained a strong reputation for quality, consistency, and premium positioning.
That does not automatically mean Benjamin Moore is the right choice for every project. But it does help explain why it is often trusted by designers, homeowners, and painting professionals looking for a higher-end result.

## Benjamin Moore vs. Sherwin-Williams vs. Behr
These are three of the brands homeowners compare most often, but they are not all positioned the same way.

### Benjamin Moore
Benjamin Moore is known for rich color, premium finishes, and a more elevated residential look. It is often favored by homeowners and designers who care about how the final result feels, not just how fast the project gets done.

### Sherwin-Williams
Sherwin-Williams is widely trusted by contractors because of its broad product systems, strong performance across many applications, and dependable exterior options. It is often a go-to choice for demanding repaint projects and long-term durability.

### Behr
Behr is known for accessibility, affordability, and convenience. It is widely recognized because of Home Depot and is often a familiar option for DIY projects and value-conscious updates.
The important point is this:
These brands are not interchangeable just because they all sell paint.
The best choice depends on what you are painting, how durable the finish needs to be, what kind of look you want, and how long you want it to last.
