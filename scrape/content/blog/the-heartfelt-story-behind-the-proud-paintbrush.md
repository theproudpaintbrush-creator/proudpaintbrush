### From Vancouver to Sugar Land: A Story of Faith, Family, and Fresh Paint

Hi, I’m Chris Petkau, owner of The Proud Paintbrush. Originally from Vancouver, Canada, I moved to the U.S. in 2007, eventually planting roots here in beautiful Sugar Land, Texas. Growing up in construction and inspired by my dad’s concrete company, I decided to pursue my own dream: launching a residential painting business that homeowners could truly rely on.
Starting a painting company in Sugar Land wasn’t easy—especially while my wife Sarah and I were also beginning our journey through IVF. But through it all, we leaned on faith, supported one another, and stayed focused. Today, we’re the proud parents of our son Isaac—and yes, we’re currently on the road toward welcoming baby number two.

### The Growth of a Painting Company That Cares
What began as a one-man operation—just me, a brush, and a dream—has grown into a multiple-crew painting company serving hundreds of homes across Sugar Land and Fort Bend County. We specialize in everything from interior painting and exterior painting to drywall repair, cabinet painting, fence painting, and even popcorn ceiling removal.
When we say we’re “Proud,” we mean it.

- We want to work with homeowners who take pride in their homes.

- We create an environment where our employees feel proud to work.

- We deliver paint jobs that clients proudly show off to friends and family.

- And we help the entire Sugar Land community look better, one home at a time.
