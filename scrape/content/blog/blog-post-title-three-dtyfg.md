# Is It Time To Update Your Fireplace?

Jan 30

Written By Chris Petkau

Looking for limewash painters in Sugar Land, TX? Whether you're updating your fireplace or refreshing the look of your living room, limewashing brick is a timeless and eco-friendly way to transform your space. Here's everything you need to know to ensure a beautiful, long-lasting finish.
What Is Limewashing?
Limewash is a natural, mineral-based paint that has been used since ancient Roman times. Unlike traditional latex paint, limewash penetrates the brick and calcifies, allowing the surface to remain breathable and porous. It offers a soft, matte finish and an authentic Old World look without the plastic feel of modern paints.
In this article, we use Romabio Limewash in Avorio White, a neutral shade that beautifully complements both warm and cool-toned interiors. We suggest learning more about lighting, undertones, warm and cool tones and perhap get a color consultation before deciding on your color.

Step 1: Clean and Prep The Surface

Before painting, it's crucial to clean the brick thoroughly. Use a wire brush with warm, soapy water. For tougher stains, apply trisodium phosphate (TSP). Skipping this step may cause imperfections to show through your finished paint job.

Step 2: Apply the paint in stages

Limewash must be diluted before application. Mix 1 part limewash with 2 parts water. Spray the brick with water, then use a heavy-nap roller followed by a masonry brush to fill in gaps and mortar joints. This ensures even coverage and a seamless look.

Get Your Desired Look

After each application phase, use a damp cloth to distress the surface for a more weathered, rustic appearance. If you prefer a cleaner, more solid finish, you can skip distressing altogether.

Allow to Cure

Let each coat cure for 4–6 hours before additional distressing. Full curing takes about 48 hours, at which point the limewash becomes as durable as natural stone. For exterior projects, limewash can be safely removed with a pressure washer within the first few days if you change your mind.
