# "Painting vs. Flooring: Which Should Come First in Your Renovation?"

Jun 24

Written By Chris Petkau

When planning a home renovation, a common question is whether to tackle interior painting before or after redoing your floors. Both tasks are significant investments, and the sequence can impact the overall outcome, timeline, and cost. In this blog, we'll explore the pros and cons of painting your interior before redoing your floors, helping you make an informed decision that best suits your renovation needs. Here is our answer: paint before flooring is installed but after demolition so no dust gets in the paint!

The Case for Interior Painting Before Flooring

1. **Avoiding Damage to New Floors**: Interior painting can be messy. Despite using drop cloths and painter’s tape, there's always a risk of paint splatters and spills. By painting your interior before installing new floors, you eliminate the worry of damaging your pristine, new flooring with paint stains or scuffs from ladders and tools.

2. **Ease of Movement**: Without new flooring to worry about, you have greater freedom to move ladders, paint cans, and other equipment around the room. This can make the interior painting process more efficient and less stressful.
3. **Seamless Edges**: Painting first allows you to paint the baseboards, trim, and walls without worrying about getting paint on your new floors. Once the floors are installed, you can add touch-ups if needed, ensuring a clean, professional finish.
4. **Time and Cost Efficiency**: If you’re hiring professional interior painting services, they might be able to complete the job quicker and more cost-effectively in an empty room without having to protect new flooring. This can reduce labor costs and project duration.

Tips for a Successful Renovation Sequence

1. **Protective Measures**: Regardless of the sequence you choose, protecting your existing surfaces is crucial. Use high-quality drop cloths, painter’s tape, and protective coverings for furniture and fixtures.

2. **Professional Advice**: Consult with both interior painting contractors and flooring professionals. They can provide valuable insights based on their experience and help you determine the best order of operations for your specific project.
3. **Consider the Entire Space**: Think about how the renovation sequence will impact the flow of your home. For example, if you’re renovating multiple rooms, you might want to stagger the interior painting and flooring to allow for livable space during the project.
4. **Test Colors and Materials**: If you decide to paint first, consider getting samples of your flooring to see how they look with your chosen paint color. Similarly, if you install floors first, test paint samples to ensure they harmonize with the new floors.

Conclusion

Ultimately, whether you choose to paint your interior before or after redoing your floors depends on your specific circumstances and preferences. Painting first can protect your new floors from damage and provide a cleaner finish, while installing floors first can prevent damage to freshly painted walls and help you make more informed color choices. By considering the pros and cons of each approach and taking protective measures, you can ensure a smooth and successful renovation process.

Whether you need residential interior painting, commercial interior painting, or custom interior painting, careful planning and coordination with professional interior painting services will help you achieve the beautiful, updated home you envision. Happy renovating!
