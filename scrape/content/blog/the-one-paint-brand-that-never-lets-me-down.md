# The One Paint Brand That Never Lets Me Down

Feb 26

Written By Chris Petkau

Sherwin Williams

When it comes to choosing the best paint brand for contractors, I stick with Sherwin-Williams over other brands like Benjamin Moore, PPG, Home Depot, or Lowe’s. It’s not necessarily about Sherwin-Williams paint quality or price—because honestly, the differences in those areas exist but are pretty negligible—but more about reliability, efficiency, and simplicity in running my business.

Product Availability & Convenience
Sherwin-Williams simply has more stores, which means I can always find what I need quickly. If I run out of something or need to make a last-minute change, I’m never far from a store that can supply me. Plus, Sherwin-Williams delivery service offers free deliveries within 24 hours, ensuring my team has what we need to keep projects moving. Other stores might have similar delivery options, but in my experience, they often don’t accommodate smaller orders or deliver in a timely manner. This is why Sherwin-Williams vs Home Depot paint is an easy decision for me.

Customer Service Matters

One of my biggest frustrations with Home Depot and Lowe’s is how difficult it is to get immediate help in the paint department. I’d love to give them more of my business because they carry other materials I use, but when it comes to paint, I can’t afford to waste time tracking down an associate. Sherwin-Williams, on the other hand, has knowledgeable staff ready to assist right away, making my job much easier. This is a huge reason why professional painters prefer Sherwin-Williams over Benjamin Moore paint or PPG paint.

Acknowledging Benjamin Moore
I’ll give Benjamin Moore credit in two areas: their color selection and custom color matching. They have some stunning palettes that really stand out, and when it comes to special requests, they do an excellent job. Occasionally, I’ll use Benjamin Moore for those reasons. However, Sherwin-Williams has a vast database of Benjamin Moore colors, meaning my clients can still get their preferred shade but with the products I trust. The ability to get Benjamin Moore custom colors while using Sherwin-Williams products makes my work even more seamless.

A Fortune 500 Powerhouse

Sherwin-Williams isn’t just another paint brand—it’s an industry leader, ranking 178th on the Fortune 500 list in 2023. That level of success speaks to their commitment to excellence and innovation, which translates into the high level of service and reliability I experience as a contractor. When considering Sherwin-Williams vs PPG paint or Sherwin-Williams vs Benjamin Moore, I find that Sherwin-Williams continues to dominate the professional painting supplies market.

Keeping Business Simple
At the end of the day, running a business is about removing complexity. My team knows Sherwin-Williams products inside and out, which means we can guarantee a flawless finish every time. Sticking with one brand streamlines our process, ensures consistency, and ultimately delivers the best results for our clients. Whether it’s choosing the best paint for interior walls or ensuring long-lasting results, I’ve found that Sherwin-Williams is the best paint brand for professional painters and homeowners alike.
So in the spirit of keeping things simple, here is what I use:

- Exteriors: Super Paint Exterior or Duration Exterior

- Interior Walls: Super Paint Interior or Duration Interior

- Ceilings: Premium Ceiling Paint

- Trim: Pro Classic Alkyd Enamel or Pro Classic Waterborne Enamel

- Cabinets: Gallery or Emerald Urethane

All from Sherwin-Williams.
