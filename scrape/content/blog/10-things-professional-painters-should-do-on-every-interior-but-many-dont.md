# 10 Things Professional Interior Painters Should Do on Every Interior (But Many Don’t)

Jan 12

Written By Chris Petkau

Hiring a painter shouldn’t feel like a gamble.
Yet many homeowners in Sugar Land and Fort Bend County only realize the difference between a cheap painter and a professional painting company after damage is done, corners are cut, or details are missed.
At The Proud Paintbrush, we believe great results come from consistent standards — not shortcuts.
Here are 10 non-negotiable things professional painters should do on every job, and why they matter to your home.

## 1. Fully Protect Floors, Furniture, and Surfaces

Professional interior painting in Sugar Land always starts with protection.
This includes:

- Drop cloths and plastic sheeting

- Furniture pads

- Taped seams with no gaps

Paint can be fixed. Scratched floors and stained carpet are much harder — and more expensive — to repair.

## 2. Ask Before Moving Anything Valuable
A professional painter never assumes.
If something looks fragile, expensive, or sentimental, we:

- Pause the job

- Ask the homeowner

- Move it carefully or work around it

Respecting your belongings is part of respecting your home.

## 3. Mask With Precision — Not Speed
Clean paint lines don’t happen by luck.
We take time to properly mask:

- Baseboards

- Door and window trim

- Light fixtures

- Ceiling fans (without taping blades)

This is one of the biggest differences between Sugar Land home painters who rush and those who take pride in their work.

## 4. Keep All Hardware Organized

Lost screws and stripped hardware are a common frustration homeowners face.
Professional painters:

- Remove hardware carefully

- Store it in labeled containers

- Reinstall everything correctly

Your home should go back together exactly how it came apart — just refreshed.

## 5. Remove and Reinstall Faceplates Correctly
Painted-over outlets and switches instantly lower the quality of a paint job.
We always:

- Remove faceplates

- Paint cleanly around outlets

- Reinstall without overtightening

It’s a small detail — and one homeowners notice immediately.

## 6. Handle Blinds and Window Treatments With Care
Blinds are often damaged by careless crews.
Professional painters:

- Remove valances properly

- Secure cords neatly

- Store blinds safely

- Reinstall them facing the correct direction

This is one of those house painting tips most companies never mention — but homeowners appreciate.

## 7. Clean as the Job Progresses

A clean job site reflects a professional mindset.
We use:

- Shop vacuums for debris

- Fine vacuums for dust

- Clean filters and tools

This keeps your home livable during the project and improves paint adhesion and finish quality.

## 8. Secure Bathroom Fixtures and Accessories
Loose or crooked fixtures are a sign of rushed work.
Professional painters ensure:

- Shower rods are straight

- Rubber pads are seated properly

- Fixtures are secure and undamaged

The goal is to leave every room better than we found it.

## 9. Cover Everything — Completely
Partial coverage leads to problems.
We make sure:

- No exposed furniture

- No uncovered counters

- No gaps in plastic or tape

If it’s not being painted, it’s being protected. Every time.

## 10. Treat the Home Like It’s Our Own
This is the standard behind everything we do.
Professional painters:

- Move carefully

- Communicate clearly

- Respect the homeowner’s space

Paint quality matters — but how your home is treated matters just as much.

### What makes a painter truly professional?
