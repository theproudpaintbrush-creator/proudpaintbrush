# 5 Red Flags When Comparing Painting Companies in Sugar Land

Sep 29

Written By Chris Petkau

## Why Homeowners Need to Look for Warning Signs
Hiring a painter in Sugar Land isn’t just about price—it’s about trust. A bad choice can leave you with peeling paint, surprise costs, or even unfinished projects. That’s why it pays to know the red flags before signing a contract.

## 1. No Written Warranty

Some painters promise “we’ll take care of you” but fail to provide a written warranty. This leaves you exposed if something goes wrong.

- Franchise example: CertaPro offers a 2-year warranty (bbb.org). That 1 Painter promotes a 3-year transferable warranty (that1painter.com).

- Local best practice: At The Proud Paintbrush, every project includes a 2-year workmanship warranty with an optional 4-year upgrade (/warranty).

If a painter won’t put it in writing, that’s a red flag.

## 2. Inconsistent Reviews
Google reviews tell the real story. Be wary if you see:

- Lots of reviews averaging 4.6–4.7 with complaints sprinkled in.

- Long gaps without recent reviews (over 6 months).

The Proud Paintbrush currently holds 88 perfect 5-star Google reviews, a sign of consistent trust. CertaPro and That 1 Painter have more volume, but their averages sit closer to 4.8.

## 3. Vague or Hidden Pricing

If a company won’t explain how they price projects—or worse, keeps everything verbal—you risk surprise costs later.
At The Proud Paintbrush, we publish pricing FAQs and blogs to help homeowners budget with confidence. Clear numbers show professionalism.

## 4. No Local Presence
Many franchise painters look local but aren’t deeply rooted in Sugar Land. Ask yourself:

- Do they support community programs?

- Is the owner involved locally, or just running a branch of a bigger system?

The Proud Paintbrush is based right here in Sugar Land, investing back into the community through programs like Paint It Forward.

## 5. Poor Communication
Unanswered calls, vague timelines, or unclear contracts are red flags. Homeowners often complain about this with lower-rated painters.
By contrast, The Proud Paintbrush has review after review praising clear communication and responsiveness. That’s one reason our 5.0 rating holds strong.

## What This Means for Homeowners

If you see these red flags—no warranty, inconsistent reviews, vague pricing, lack of local roots, or poor communication—think twice before hiring.
The Proud Paintbrush avoids these pitfalls with clear warranties, perfect reviews, transparent pricing, and community commitment. That’s why Sugar Land homeowners trust us.

## FAQ
Q: What’s the #1 red flag to watch for?
A missing or unclear warranty. If it’s not in writing, you’re at risk.
Q: Are reviews more important than price?
Yes—bad reviews cost more in the long run than a fair, transparent price.
Q: Why do franchises get more reviews?
They use structured systems to request them, but not all are 5-star consistent.
Q: How can I be sure a painter is truly local?
Look for community involvement, local testimonials, and an owner you can meet.
