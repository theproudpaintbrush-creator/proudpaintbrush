# Oh, No… Rust! What to Do Before You Paint Over Rust

Nov 7

Written By Chris Petkau

Prepping for Exterior Painting: Essential Tips
When it comes to exterior painting, whether it's furniture, walls, or metal objects, proper pre-planning is key to achieving the best results. Ensuring that the surface is ready for the paint job is crucial, whether you're painting an exterior wall, a metal chair, or an outdoor shed. Following the right steps, as recommended by professional exterior painters, will make your project smoother and more successful.

Yes, You Can Paint Over Rust!

Preparing to paint a metal object with rust? It’s easier than you think to paint over rust if you use the right tools and methods. Start by cleaning the item thoroughly using a wire brush to remove as much rust as possible. For flaky rust, brush off the flakes to achieve a smooth surface. After scraping off the rust, use sandpaper to smooth out any remaining rough spots. Clean the entire item with a strong detergent and let it dry naturally. Once dry, your object is ready for painting. Choosing the right type of paint is the next crucial step.

Choosing the Right Paint
Many items, especially metal ones, respond well to spray paints after proper cleaning and rust removal. Always check the paint manufacturer’s directions to see if a rust-blocking primer is needed. When spray painting, apply an even, smooth coat to ensure a professional finish. If necessary, apply a second coat if the first one doesn’t look right. Thorough preparation ensures that the spray paint adheres well and looks great. Alternatively, using a brush and regular paint can also yield excellent results, provided the item is properly prepped.

Additional Tips for Exterior Painting

Removing rust and prepping the surface is easier than most people think. A smooth, rust-free surface is essential before you begin painting. Follow the product instructions exactly to ensure success. The extra effort in preparing the item will be well worth it when you see the final results. 

Final Thoughts
Exterior painting projects require careful planning and preparation. Whether you’re working on house exteriors, furniture, or metal objects, following these steps will help you achieve a professional and lasting finish. Remember, the key to a successful paint job lies in the preparation. So take the time to get it right, and your exterior painting project will look fantastic for years to come.
