# 💰 How Much Do Painting Companies Charge? A Breakdown for Homeowners

Jul 21

Written By Chris Petkau

If you're planning a painting project and wondering,
"How much do painting companies charge?"
you’re not alone.

Whether you're repainting a single room or your entire house, understanding average painting costs can help you budget smarter and avoid surprises.
At The Proud Paintbrush, we believe in transparency, quality, and fair pricing — so we’ve created this guide to help you understand what really goes into the cost of a professional paint job.

## 🧾 Average Cost of Hiring a Painting Company

Here’s what most homeowners can expect to pay for professional painting services:

- Interior walls: $2.00 – $4.50 per square foot

- Ceilings: $0.80 – $1.50 per square foot

- Exterior painting: $1.50 – $3.50 per square foot

- Cabinet painting: $80 – $120 per door

- Trim & baseboards: $1.25 – $2.50 per linear foot

- Interior doors: $70 – $120 each

These ranges can vary based on prep work, surface condition, and finish type.

## 🧱 What Affects the Final Price?

### 🎨 1. Square Footage
The larger the surface area, the more time, paint, and labor it requires.

### 🛠️ 2. Prep Work
Cleaning, sanding, caulking, and patching drywall all add time — and ensure a longer-lasting finish.

### 🌈 3. Paint Quality
Using premium paints like Sherwin-Williams Emerald or Benjamin Moore Regal Select can raise material costs, but improve durability and appearance.

### 🖌️ 4. Coats Needed
Painting over dark colors or rough surfaces may require two or more coats, especially if you’re switching finishes.

### 🏡 5. Interior vs. Exterior
Exterior jobs may involve special equipment (scaffolding, pressure washers), increasing cost.

## 📍 Local Pricing in Sugar Land & Fort Bend County

In the Sugar Land area, here’s a rough guide:

- Full interior repaint: $3,500 – $9,000

- Exterior repaint: $4,000 – $12,000

- Kitchen cabinet refinishing: $2,000 – $5,000

Prices vary by home size, number of rooms, condition of walls, and number of stories.
