## Our Interior Painting Process (This Is Where Most Companies Fall Short)
A paint job doesn’t fail because of paint.
It fails because of what was skipped before the paint went on.
Here’s how we prevent that:

### 1. Home Protection Comes First
We cover floors, protect furniture, and mask surfaces before anything starts.
This isn’t optional—it’s part of doing the job right.

### 2. Surface Prep & Repairs
We address:

- nail holes

- drywall imperfections

- cracks and seams

If needed, we include drywall repair services so the final finish isn’t compromised.

### 3. Sanding & Surface Conditioning
This is one of the most skipped steps in the industry.
We sand to create proper adhesion and eliminate texture inconsistencies.

### 4. Priming Where It Matters
Not every surface needs primer—but the ones that do will fail without it.
We apply the right products where they’re actually needed.

### 5. Precision Application
We apply coatings evenly using the correct tools and techniques to avoid:

- lap marks

- flashing

- uneven sheen

### 6. Cleanup & Final Walkthrough
We leave your home clean—and we walk the project with you to make sure everything meets expectations.
👉 This process is the difference between a paint job that looks good for a few months and one that holds up for years.
