## ❓ Cabinet Painting FAQs
What is the best paint brand for painting cabinets?
Sherwin-Williams Gallery Series. It’s a water-based enamel with a super durable and beautiful finish.
How long does it take to have my cabinets painted?
Large kitchens take 3–5 days. Smaller vanities can be done in 1–2. It depends on prep, primer, and drying time.
What is the best time of year to paint cabinets?
Any time! Cabinet painting is done indoors, so seasons don’t matter. Our low-VOC products are kid- and pet-safe.
How do you prep cabinets for painting?
We clean, degrease, sand, caulk, prime, and mask the space. Doors and drawers are removed for even finishes.
Can I paint my cabinets?
Yes, but pro results are tough to replicate. We use premium products and HVLP sprayers for a flawless finish.
What sheen is best for painting cabinets?
Satin or semi-gloss. Both are durable, cleanable, and elevate the look.
Is it cheaper to paint or replace my cabinets?
Painting is dramatically cheaper than replacing and still offers a showroom-quality result.
What colors are best for resale value?
White, greige, taupe, and sage. Neutral, modern tones help buyers envision their own style.
How do I avoid peeling paint?
Prep is everything. We sand, clean, prime, and use cabinet-grade enamel to prevent failure.
What tools do pros use?
We use HVLP fine finish sprayers after priming. It’s still the best method for smooth, brush-free results.
