## Our Exterior Painting Process
Every exterior painting project follows a process designed to improve adhesion, protect surfaces, and produce a finish that lasts.

### 1. Consultation
We review the home, identify the surfaces involved, discuss your goals, and evaluate the condition of the existing coating and substrate before work begins.

### 2. Cleaning and Preparation
Exterior painting starts with proper preparation. Depending on the project, that can include washing, scraping, sanding, caulking, masking, and surface repairs.

### 3. Surface Repairs and Priming
When needed, we address damaged areas, repair minor surface issues, and apply the appropriate primer so the coating system has a strong foundation.

### 4. Painting
We apply paint or stain using the right methods, tools, and products for the surface being coated. Our focus is on even coverage, clean finish quality, and long-term durability.

### 5. Final Walkthrough
Before wrapping up, we review the work with you to make sure the details are complete and the project meets expectations.
A strong process helps reduce common exterior paint failures like peeling, early fading, poor adhesion, and uneven coverage.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Exterior Painting

### How often should a home exterior be repainted?
That depends on the surface type, product used, previous prep quality, sun exposure, and overall weather conditions. Some homes need repainting sooner than others based on wear and material type.

### Do you pressure wash before exterior painting?
In many cases, yes. Proper cleaning is an important part of exterior preparation and helps the new coating adhere better.

### Can you paint stucco, brick, and siding?
Yes. We work on a variety of exterior surfaces, including stucco, brick, wood, trim, cement fiber siding, and other common residential materials.

### Do you help identify damaged areas before painting?
Yes. During the estimate and prep process, we evaluate the condition of the surfaces and identify issues that may need repair before painting.

### Do you offer staining for fences and decks?
Yes. Fence and deck staining can often be included as part of a larger exterior project or handled as a standalone service when appropriate.
