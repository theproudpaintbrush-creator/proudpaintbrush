Our warranty is designed to protect against workmanship-related issues that occur during the coverage period.

- Adhesion failure related to workmanship

# What Is Not Covered
Like all professional painting warranties, certain conditions fall outside workmanship coverage.
Examples may include:

- Structural movement or settling

- Water intrusion or leaks

- Roof or plumbing failures

- Mold or moisture issues originating behind surfaces

- Normal wear and tear

- Physical damage or abuse

- Fading caused by long-term UV exposure

- Manufacturer product defects

- Surfaces previously coated improperly by others

We always aim to explain expectations clearly before work begins so homeowners understand how to best protect their investment.

# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you actually honor your painting warranty?
Yes. If a workmanship-related issue occurs during the warranty period, we’ll inspect the concern, communicate clearly, and work toward a resolution.

## What voids a painting warranty?
Things like water leaks, structural movement, physical damage, excessive moisture, or improper maintenance can affect warranty coverage. We explain expectations before every project.

## How long should a professional paint job last?
Most professionally painted interiors and exteriors can last many years depending on preparation quality, product selection, environmental exposure, and maintenance.

## Is exterior paint harder on homes in Texas?
Yes. Texas homes deal with intense heat, UV exposure, humidity, and severe weather. That’s why prep work and high-quality exterior coatings are especially important.

## Do you use premium paint products?
Yes. We offer upgraded paint systems designed for improved durability, washability, color retention, and long-term performance.

## Are touch-ups included?
For qualifying workmanship-related concerns during the warranty period, touch-ups may be included depending on the situation and package selected.
