# Our Painting Process

## Protect
Before work begins, we protect floors, furniture, landscaping, fixtures, and surrounding surfaces to minimize disruption and keep the job site clean.

## Prepare
Preparation is one of the biggest factors in long-term paint performance.
Depending on the project this may include:
• Masking and protection
• Sanding and surface preparation
• Drywall repairs
• Caulking gaps and joints
• Spot priming or full priming
• Texture repairs
• Exterior washing and prep

## Paint
Products and application methods are selected based on the project requirements.
We focus on clean lines, proper coverage, durability, and consistent finishes.

## Inspect
Projects are supervised throughout production and reviewed regularly to maintain quality standards.

## Final Walkthrough
Every project ends with a walkthrough to confirm the completed work and address any final touch-ups.

# Common Questions

## Will painting make the house messy?
Painting can temporarily look messy during masking, sanding, repairs, and spraying. This is normal and helps produce the finished result.

## Can we stay in the house?
Most interior projects remain occupied. We coordinate room sequencing whenever possible.

## Do I need to move furniture?
Light decluttering helps.
Large items can often remain in place and be protected, though advance notice helps with planning.

## What about pets?
Pets should be secured during work to help keep doors accessible and everyone safe.
