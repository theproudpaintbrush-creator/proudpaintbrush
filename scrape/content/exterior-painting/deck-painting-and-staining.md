### FAQs – Deck Painting & Staining in Sugar Land
Q1: How often should I stain or paint my deck in Sugar Land?
A: In Texas weather, decks typically need re-staining every 2–3 years and repainting every 5–7 years, depending on wear.
Q2: Which is better—painting or staining a deck?
A: Staining penetrates the wood for a natural look, while paint offers more color options and stronger surface protection.
Q3: How long does the process take?
A: Most residential deck projects take 1–3 days, depending on deck size, prep work, and weather conditions.
Q4: Do you offer eco-friendly options?
A: Yes! We have low-VOC paints and stains that are safer for your family, pets, and the environment.
