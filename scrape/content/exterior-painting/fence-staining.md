## Frequently Asked Questions
How often should I stain my fence?
Most fences need staining every 2–3 years due to sun and humidity.
What type of stain lasts longest?
Oil-based stains penetrate deeper and last longer, making them ideal for our climate.
Do you clean the fence before staining?
Yes. Every project includes power washing and prep to ensure proper adhesion and an even finish.
Can you match my fence color?
Absolutely. We offer a variety of colors and finishes to complement your home and landscape.
