### PAA-Style FAQs
Q1: Can you paint Hardie Board siding in Sugar Land?
Yes. Hardie Board is a brand of fiber cement siding and can be painted using high-quality exterior acrylic paints for a long-lasting finish.
Q2: How often should fiber cement siding be painted?
On average, every 10–15 years depending on paint quality, sun exposure, and maintenance.
Q3: Do you need to prime fiber cement siding before painting?
Not always — newer, factory-primed boards may be ready for paint. Older or unpainted boards benefit from a bonding primer.
Q4: What’s the best paint for fiber cement siding in Texas?
100% acrylic exterior paints from brands like Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore perform best in Texas heat and humidity.
