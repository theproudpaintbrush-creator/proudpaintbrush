### Can You Paint Vinyl Siding a Darker Color?
Yes—but only with vinyl-safe formulas. Dark colors absorb more heat, so using the wrong paint can cause warping. That’s why we carefully check your desired color against manufacturer recommendations and only apply products proven safe for vinyl.
