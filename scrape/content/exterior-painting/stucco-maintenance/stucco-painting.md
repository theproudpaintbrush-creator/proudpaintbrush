## FAQs About Stucco Painting in Sugar Land
1. How often should stucco be painted in Sugar Land?
Most stucco homes should be repainted every 7–10 years, but in Sugar Land’s climate, regular inspections are key to maintaining protection.
2. What type of paint works best for stucco?
Elastomeric paint or 100% acrylic latex paint is recommended for stucco because it can expand and contract with the surface.
3. Can you fix stucco cracks before painting?
Yes! Repairing cracks and damage is part of our preparation process before any paint is applied.

Absolutely. A freshly painted stucco exterior improves curb appeal and adds resale value.
