# Pressure Washing – The Proud Paintbrush

## What Pressure Washing Services Do We Offer?
At The Proud Paintbrush, we provide expert pressure washing to remove dirt, mildew, algae, and old paint from all types of exterior surfaces. This essential step ensures a smooth, long-lasting finish for your home or business, keeping your property looking fresh and protected year-round.

## Why Pressure Washing Matters for Your Home
Proper pressure washing is one of the most important steps in preparing your exterior for painting or staining. In areas with heat, humidity, or heavy rainfall, mildew, chalky residue, and dirt can build up on siding, stucco, brick, or wood. If these contaminants aren’t removed, paint or stain won’t adhere properly, leading to peeling, blistering, or premature wear.
Our professional-grade pressure washing equipment and techniques safely remove these buildups, providing a clean surface that extends the life of your paint or stain.

## Our Pressure Washing Process
When you choose The Proud Paintbrush, pressure washing is included as part of our exterior prep services:
1. Inspection – We evaluate your property for dirt, mildew, loose paint, and other problem areas.
2. Protective Setup – Sensitive areas like windows, doors, landscaping, and outdoor furniture are covered to prevent damage.
3. Precision Cleaning – We use the correct pressure and techniques for siding, stucco, brick, fascia, eaves, and trim.
4. Final Rinse – Surfaces are rinsed thoroughly and allowed to dry completely before painting or staining begins.

## Frequently Asked Questions
1. Do I need to book pressure washing separately?
No. Pressure washing is automatically included as part of our exterior painting or staining preparation.
2. How long after pressure washing can painting or staining begin?
Most surfaces need 24–48 hours to dry completely before the next step.
3. Can pressure washing damage my property?
No. Our team carefully adjusts pressure levels and uses techniques designed to safely clean all exterior surfaces.
4. Do you offer stand-alone pressure washing services?
Yes. While most pressure washing is part of a painting project, we occasionally provide standalone service.
