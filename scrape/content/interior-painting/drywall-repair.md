### Our Drywall Repair Process

- Assessment: We inspect cracks, holes, and damage to determine the best repair approach.

- Preparation: Damaged drywall sections are cut out and cleaned for a secure patch.

- Repair & Blending: Patching, taping, and multiple layers of joint compound are applied.

- Sanding & Finishing: We sand smooth, prime, and prepare the surface for a perfect paint finish.

- Painting (Optional): Complete the job with matching paint for a fresh, flawless look.

### Common Drywall Issues We Fix

- Holes from door handles or accidents

- Water damage and stains

- Nail pops and screw holes

- Settlement cracks around doors, windows, or ceilings

- Seam gaps and uneven textures
