#### Why Bathroom Painting Requires Special Expertise
Bathrooms present unique challenges for painters. High humidity, frequent temperature changes, and constant exposure to water mean that the wrong paint can peel, bubble, or mildew quickly. That’s why we use:

- Moisture-resistant paints for walls and ceilings.

- Mold- and mildew-inhibiting primers for lasting protection.

- Precision prep work to ensure smooth, even coverage.

- Sealants for trim and baseboards to prevent water damage.
