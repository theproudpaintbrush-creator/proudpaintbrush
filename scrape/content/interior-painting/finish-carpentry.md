## What is Finish Carpentry?
Finish carpentry is the stage of construction that transforms a bare structure into a polished, livable space. It involves installing the decorative and functional wood elements you see and touch every day—such as baseboards, crown molding, door casings, window trim, wainscoting, and custom shelving. In Sugar Land homes, these details add warmth, style, and sophistication.
