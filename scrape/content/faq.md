## Frequently Asked Painting Questions – Fort Bend County & West Houston

### Q: Need quick answers about professional painting services?
A: We’ve compiled our most common questions from homeowners across Sugar Land, Missouri City, Richmond, Katy, and West Houston. These answers explain our process, pricing, service areas, and how The Proud Paintbrush delivers top-quality residential painting with care.

## Do You Offer Free Painting Estimates?
Yes. We offer fast, no-obligation painting estimates for homeowners across our service area.
You can schedule a time that works for you and we’ll walk through your project, answer questions, and provide a detailed quote.

## What Services Do You Offer?
The Proud Paintbrush provides a full range of professional residential painting services, including:

- Interior painting (walls, ceilings, trim, cabinets)

- Exterior painting (stucco, brick, siding, soffits)

- Drywall repair and texture matching

- Cabinet refinishing and repainting

- Fence and deck staining

- Minor carpentry and surface preparation

## How Long Does a Typical Painting Project Take?
Most interior painting projects take 2–5 days, depending on the size of the home and the level of detail involved.
Exterior painting projects typically take 3–7 days, depending on weather conditions, surface preparation needs, and the size of the property.
We communicate clearly throughout the process so you always know what to expect.

## Is Your Team Background-Checked and Insured?
Absolutely.
Every team member is background-checked, professionally trained, and fully insured. We treat every home with respect and maintain a clean, organized worksite throughout the project.

## What Brands of Paint Do You Use?
We primarily use Sherwin-Williams paints because of their durability, coverage, and extensive color selections.
We’re also happy to help homeowners choose the right color, sheen, and product for each surface.

## People Also Ask

### How do I choose the right paint color?
We offer color consultations and can help you test samples on your walls so you can confidently choose the perfect color.

### Do you paint during the winter?
Yes. Interior painting can be completed year-round.
For exterior projects, we monitor weather conditions and schedule work during appropriate temperature and humidity ranges.

### What’s the best time of year to paint a home?
Spring and fall typically offer the most ideal conditions for exterior painting, but with proper preparation and scheduling, painting projects can be completed most of the year in the Houston area.

### Do you warranty your work?
Yes. All of our work includes a written warranty and satisfaction guarantee, based on the scope of the project.
